# AKI Starter Kit (WWF No Mercy)

The example conversion from the AKI starter kit. It shows every part a conversion can have:

- **Code change** (`patch.nrm`): the intro is skipped and the attract demo (tag team) starts about 5 seconds after the title, always in the Raw arena.
- **Codes** (`codes.txt`): Championship money stays full; player 1 has full spirit in matches.
- **RT light rig** (`rt_lights.json`): teal and violet lighting in the Raw arena (turn on ray tracing to see it).
- **Seed save**: everything unlocked, applied once to this conversion's own save.
- **Textures** (package `AKI-Starter_Kit_Textures`): a new ring mat, apron logo and turnbuckle pads.
- **Models** (`models/`): eight CC0 low-poly characters in place of eight stock wrestlers, with HD textures.

Start it, leave the controller alone, and watch the demo.
